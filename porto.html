MBG Face Recognition

On-device face pipeline for MBG kitchen staff and students. Registration uses RGB auto-capture: ML Kit detection checks face size and position inside a guide oval, then five shots are taken under a cycling full-screen colour mask. A face browser manages each person's crops and embeddings. Recognition matches FaceNet-512 TensorFlow Lite embeddings by cosine similarity against each person's averaged embedding.
